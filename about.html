About

Applied machine learning, from the training notebook through to a deployed interface someone can actually use.

I'm a B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering student at Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College, Ludhiana, focused on applied machine learning — the kind that goes from a training notebook to a deployed, usable interface.

Most of my project work sits at the intersection of tabular prediction and time-series modelling: gradient-boosted trees for structured agricultural data, and recurrent architectures for continuous trajectory data.

What interests me is the part after the model converges — turning a validated architecture into a low-latency service with a real interface, and making the residual structure of its errors legible rather than hidden.
